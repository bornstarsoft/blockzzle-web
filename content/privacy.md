# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-06-08

Blockzzle (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you visit **blockzzle.com**.

## 1) What we collect

### A. Local game data
Blockzzle stores local game data in your browser, such as best score, today best, games played, last score, best lines, best clear, sound preference, and anonymous local player id.

### B. Anonymous leaderboard submissions
If you choose to submit a leaderboard score, we receive the nickname you enter, score, lines, best clear, score tier, run duration, client version, board version, local day key, submission time, and anonymous browser player id. We do not require an account.

### C. Contact messages
If you email us, we will receive the information you include (such as your email address and the contents of your message).

## 2) How we use information
We use the information to:
- operate, maintain, and improve Blockzzle
- show anonymous leaderboard scores you choose to submit
- detect abuse, prevent fraud, and maintain security
- respond to inquiries and support requests

## 3) Sharing
We do **not** sell your personal information.

Leaderboard entries may be displayed publicly using the nickname you submit. We do not publish your anonymous browser player id.

## 4) Third-party links
Blockzzle may contain links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices may differ. Please review their policies before using their services.

## 5) Children’s privacy
This website is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6) Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
