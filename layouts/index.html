How to Play Blockzzle

Blockzzle is a free online block puzzle game you can start immediately in your browser.

  • Place the block pieces on the board.
  • Complete lines to clear space and score points.
  • Plan ahead so the board does not run out of room.

Blockzzle Tips

  • Keep space open near the center.
  • Avoid filling corners too early.
  • Look for line clear opportunities before placing large shapes.
